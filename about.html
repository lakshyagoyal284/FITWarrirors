My Journey

From Transformation To Coaching

I'm Dipti — a fitness coach and movement enthusiast who believes that fitness is not about becoming smaller. It's about becoming stronger, more capable and more confident in your own body.

My own fitness journey has shaped the way I coach today. After becoming a mother, I went through a major transformation — rebuilding my strength and fitness through consistent training, structured nutrition and a lot of discipline. What started as a personal journey eventually became a passion for helping others discover what their bodies are capable of.

Over the years, I've trained in strength training, powerlifting, functional fitness, endurance running, Zumba and HYROX-style conditioning. I've competed in powerlifting and endurance events, completed HYROX, and continue to challenge myself through strength and athletic performance.
